# Chapter 1: The Window of Rain

Mira watched the rain draw silver ladders down the window. Her small paper boat waited on the table, folded from yesterday's homework. Grandpa said every boat needed a brave captain, even if the sea was only a puddle.

When the clouds opened wider, Mira tucked the boat under her coat and stepped outside. The street smelled like warm stones and wet leaves. At the curb, a bright puddle shivered as if it had been waiting for her.

Mira placed the boat on the water. It spun once, twice, then sailed toward the gutter with the confidence of a tiny moon. Mira ran beside it, laughing, while Grandpa followed with the red umbrella.

# Chapter 2: The Lantern Under the Bridge

The paper boat slipped beneath the footbridge where the world became green and echoing. Mira crouched low and saw a lantern glowing under the arch. Beside it sat Niko, a very serious frog wearing a bottle-cap hat.

Niko explained that the lantern was for lost wishes. Every time rain fell, wishes floated down the street, and someone had to guide them home before night. Mira looked at her boat. It suddenly seemed exactly the right size for the job.

Together they loaded three wishes into the paper boat: a blue wish for courage, a yellow wish for soup, and a quiet wish that hummed like a sleepy bee. The boat bobbed, heavy but proud.

# Chapter 3: Home Before Moonrise

By moonrise, the rain had softened to mist. Mira, Niko, and Grandpa followed the boat to the garden gate, where the three wishes lifted into the air like tiny stars. The yellow wish drifted into Grandpa's kitchen window.

Soon the house smelled of soup, and Mira wrapped the damp paper boat in a towel. Niko saluted from the flowerpot. He promised that the next rain would bring another voyage, and perhaps a map.

Mira fell asleep with the red umbrella by her bed and the sound of rain tapping a gentle secret on the roof.
